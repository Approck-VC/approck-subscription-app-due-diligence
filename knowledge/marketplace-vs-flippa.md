# Specialized vs. General Marketplaces

> Published at [approck.com/knowledge/marketplace-vs-flippa](https://approck.com/knowledge/marketplace-vs-flippa)

## General Marketplaces (Flippa, Empire Flippers, etc.)

General-purpose digital asset marketplaces list everything from blogs and SaaS products to e-commerce stores and mobile apps. They serve the broadest possible market.

**Pros:** Large buyer pool, established brand, wide variety of listings.

**Cons:**

- No specialized expertise in mobile app subscriptions — listings aren't deeply vetted for subscription-specific metrics
- High commissions (often 10–15%+)
- Mixed quality — serious apps listed alongside low-quality or misleading listings
- No guidance on subscription-specific valuation, ASO analysis, or cohort modeling
- Buyer must bring their own expertise or risk overpaying

## Specialized Subscription App Marketplaces

Niche platforms focused exclusively on subscription-based mobile applications operate differently. They combine marketplace functionality with domain expertise.

**Pros:**

- Deep verification — every listing is analyzed for MRR accuracy, traffic source integrity, and revenue sustainability
- Expert guidance — marketplace managers understand subscription metrics, cohort analysis, and fair pricing
- Curated listings — only apps that pass due diligence are listed, reducing the buyer's risk
- Negotiation support — managers help both sides reach a fair deal, balancing seller expectations with buyer economics
- Escrow/guarantee services built in
- Lower or seller-only commissions (typically 5–10%)

> **Analogy**
>
> Think of the difference like buying a commercial truck. A general-purpose vehicle marketplace (like Craigslist) might have listings, but you're on your own for inspection, pricing, and negotiation. A specialized commercial truck dealer knows the fleet business — they'll help you calculate ROI, check maintenance records, and match you with the right vehicle for your routes. The specialized platform costs the seller a fee but creates significantly better outcomes for both parties.


## Frequently asked questions

### How is a specialized app marketplace different from a general one?

Specialized marketplaces typically offer deeper subscription-focused vetting, pricing guidance, and transfer support.

### What is a common drawback of general marketplaces?

Listing quality and mobile-specific due diligence can vary widely, increasing buyer workload and execution risk.

### Who benefits most from specialized platforms?

Both buyers and sellers who want curated listings, domain expertise, and smoother app-specific transaction workflows.
