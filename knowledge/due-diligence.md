# Due Diligence Checklist for Buying an App

> Published at [approck.com/knowledge/due-diligence](https://approck.com/knowledge/due-diligence)

## Financial Verification
- **Access App Store Connect / Google Play Console.** Verify reported MRR directly in the platform's financial reports — never rely solely on screenshots.
- **Cross-reference with payment analytics.** Services like RevenueCat, Adapty, Apphud, or Qonversion provide independent subscription tracking. Compare their numbers with platform reports.
- **Check net vs. gross revenue.** Confirm whether the seller is on the 15% (Small Business Program) or 30% commission tier.
- **Analyze revenue trends.** Look at the last 6–12 months. Is MRR stable, growing, or declining? What's driving the trend?

## Traffic & Acquisition History
- **When was the last marketing campaign?** The longer since the last paid acquisition, the more reliable current organic revenue is. Apps with marketing running in the last 3–6 months carry higher forecasting risk.
- **What were the traffic sources?** Facebook Ads, Google UAC, TikTok, ASO, organic search — each source produces subscribers with different retention profiles.
- **Were motivational (incentivized) installs used?** Incentivized installs inflate download numbers but produce low-quality subscribers who churn quickly.
- **Verify install sources.** While 100% verification is impossible, approximately 90% of traffic sources can be confirmed through analytics tools and platform data.

## Technical & Legal Review
- **Account history.** Has the app been transferred between developer accounts? Each transfer resets some trust signals with Apple.
- **Account type.** Is it registered under an individual or a legal entity? Entity accounts are generally preferred for larger transactions.
- **Source code.** Obtain the latest working build, design files, and documentation. Verify the code compiles and matches the live App Store version.
- **Privacy policies & compliance.** Ensure privacy policies are current. Apple may flag outdated policies during the next review.
- **App Store review history.** Check for any past rejections, warnings, or policy violations.

## Seller Conversation
- **Conduct a live call with the developer.** Ask about the app's history, why they're selling, any known issues, and their honest assessment of future prospects.
- **Verify ownership.** Confirm the seller actually owns the app and has authority to sell. Request proof of identity matching the developer account registration.

> **The Ideal App Profile**
>
> An app published 3+ years ago, with no paid marketing for 12+ months, never transferred between accounts, registered under the original developer's legal entity, in an evergreen category, with stable organic MRR and a clear subscriber decay curve. This profile offers the highest predictability and lowest risk.


## Frequently asked questions

### What is the first due-diligence step before buying an app?

Verify financial data directly in App Store Connect or Google Play Console rather than relying on screenshots.

### How can investors validate traffic quality?

Review acquisition history, paid vs. organic mix, and analytics evidence to spot incentivized or low-quality installs.

### What legal and technical checks are essential?

Confirm ownership, account history, source code integrity, compliance status, and transfer readiness before signing.
