# Subscription App Valuation Inputs

Use this document to assemble the evidence behind a valuation range. It does not prescribe a universal multiple: two apps with similar monthly revenue can have materially different values because of retention, traffic quality, operating effort, platform risk, and transfer complexity.

## Reporting basis

Define the following before comparing any metrics:

- Reporting currency and exchange-rate source
- Gross revenue, net store proceeds, and net operating revenue
- Monthly reporting cutoff and time zone
- Refund, tax, and platform-fee treatment
- Which apps, platforms, countries, and products are included
- Whether figures are cash-based, accrual-based, or estimates

Prepare at least 12 complete months when available. Mark partial months and one-off events rather than annualizing them silently.

## Revenue evidence

- Monthly App Store and Google Play sales or financial reports
- Subscription-platform reports from RevenueCat, Adapty, Apphud, or the system of record
- Processor reports for web subscriptions, if applicable
- Bank or accounting records that reconcile to received proceeds
- Refunds, chargebacks, taxes, and platform commissions
- Revenue split by platform, country, product, and billing period
- One-time purchases, advertising, or other non-subscription revenue shown separately

Reconcile the same dates and definitions across systems. Record unexplained differences instead of selecting the highest figure.

## Subscription health

- Active paid subscriptions at each month end
- New trials, trial-to-paid conversion, and trial duration
- New paid subscriptions and renewals
- Voluntary and involuntary churn
- Billing-period mix: weekly, monthly, annual, and lifetime
- Cohort retention by start month
- Refund and cancellation rates
- Grace-period, billing-retry, and recovered-subscription behavior
- Price changes and grandfathered plans

Separate new-business performance from the existing subscription tail. A short acquisition spike can raise current revenue without improving its durability.

## Traffic and acquisition

- Installs and store-page conversion by platform
- Organic, paid, cross-promotion, influencer, and referral shares
- Advertising spend by channel and month
- Cost per install, trial, and paid subscriber
- Payback period and contribution margin by channel
- Campaigns paused or scaled during the reporting window
- Keyword, featuring, or viral events that may not repeat
- Dependence on one advertising account, partner, or traffic source

Confirm whether reported revenue can persist without the seller's advertising access, audience, personal brand, or cross-promotion inventory.

## Operating costs

- Cloud hosting, storage, databases, and content delivery
- APIs, AI models, data providers, and other usage-based services
- Analytics, attribution, messaging, customer support, and monitoring
- Design, engineering, content, moderation, and localization
- Paid acquisition and agency fees
- Legal, accounting, insurance, and compliance costs
- Domains, email, certificates, and developer-program fees
- Owner labor currently omitted from the profit calculation

Classify each cost as fixed, variable, discretionary, or required for continued operation.

## Product and platform risk

- Revenue or installs concentrated in one country, platform, or product
- Store warnings, rejected submissions, removals, or unresolved policy issues
- Sensitive categories, regulated features, or licensed content
- Privacy, consent, age-rating, and data-retention obligations
- Third-party code, data, trademarks, or media without clear transfer rights
- Backend or vendor dependencies controlled by the seller or another business
- Material technical debt or an unsupported framework version
- Reviews, ratings, or support volume suggesting product-quality issues

## Growth evidence

Treat a growth opportunity as evidence-backed only when it has:

- A defined change or experiment
- A measurable audience or funnel stage
- Historical results, a comparable test, or another defensible basis
- An estimated cost and implementation owner
- A downside case if the result does not materialize

Do not price an untested idea as if the improvement has already happened.

## Seller data pack

A compact first-pass data pack should contain:

- Monthly profit and loss or operating statement
- Store and subscription reports using the same monthly periods
- Cohort-retention export
- Acquisition-spend and attribution summary
- Current service and infrastructure costs
- Product and release history
- Store-policy and account-history summary
- Asset, dependency, and transfer inventory

Redact end-user personal data. Buyers usually need aggregated performance evidence, not raw customer records, during initial diligence.

For a directional starting range, use the [Approck valuation tool](https://approck.com/valuation). A final price still depends on verification, transaction structure, and the risks identified during diligence.
